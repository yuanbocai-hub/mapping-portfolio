# Rights and attribution

This repository brings together research notes, modern cartographic reconstructions, and reproductions of historical maps. Their rights and source attributions are not interchangeable.

- Existing cartographer, studio, and source credits embedded in the map images have been retained.
- No project-wide open-content or open-data license has been assigned. Public availability on GitHub does not itself grant permission for unrestricted reuse.
- The historical reproductions were supplied by the project owner. Full publication and reproduction provenance should be established before making a definitive rights claim about them.
- Modern reference datasets retain their own license and attribution requirements, including OpenStreetMap’s ODbL and attribution obligations where applicable.
- Before reusing or redistributing a map, consult its embedded credits and the source notes, and obtain permission where necessary.

These statements do not change the rights in any underlying historical source or third-party dataset.
